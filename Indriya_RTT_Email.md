Quick summary:

- 60% Technical + Practical / 40% Sales — technical knowledge has to be strong, so it anchors the program; sales is a skill built over time, so it carries the remaining 40% on that foundation
- Scoped for 200 participants (2 associates per store, ~90 stores)
- Three formats, each with its own duration, batch size and fee: In-Person (2 full days, recommended — Rs. 2,500/participant, up to 30/batch), Hybrid (2 half-days virtual + 1 full day in-person — Rs. 2,250/participant, up to 30/batch), and Virtual (4 half-days — 2 technical + 2 sales — Rs. 2,000/participant, up to 60/batch). Full pros/cons of each in the attached proposal
- Technical curriculum covers the 4Cs, diamond shapes & cuts, color stone quality drivers, and treatments & disclosure, with hands-on practical handling
- Sales curriculum is a detailed, eight-module, practice-led track — Indriya's value proposition, translating technical knowledge into sales language, price & value conversations, reading the customer, objection handling, cross-sell/upsell, closing, and live role-play coaching — set out in full in the attached proposal
- As discussed, the training does not reference third-party certification with customers — it's built entirely around Indriya's own value proposition and differentiation. Associates receive an internal Certificate of Completion on finishing the program, used only for Indriya's own tracking and recognition, never with customers
- Pre-training and post-training tests, with the score delta shown live on a manager dashboard the moment a batch is scored — no separate report
- A comparable IGI training rollout delivered a +11.1% post-training revenue index lift for a leading jewelry retailer, driven entirely by in-person delivery — the same rollout's online-only sessions did not produce a comparable lift, which is why IGI recommends In-Person by default. Full context in the attached proposal
- Illustrative total investment: Rs. 4,00,000–5,00,000 (+GST) for the full 200-participant scope, depending on the format split by store — final numbers to be confirmed against your store-by-store format preference
- For In-Person and Hybrid batches, 2 IGI specialist instructors are deployed per batch (technical + sales); airfare, stay and local conveyance for both instructors to be arranged and borne by Indriya
