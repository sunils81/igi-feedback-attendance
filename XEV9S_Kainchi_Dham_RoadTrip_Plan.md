## Charging Network Notes
- **Tata Power EZ Charge** and **Statiq** have confirmed stations in Nashik, Indore, Gwalior, Agra, and Bareilly — all on this route.
- Mumbai–Pune and Mumbai–Nagpur corridors have dense fast-charging (100+ kW), but since this route goes via Nashik–Indore (not Pune–Nagpur), rely on Tata Power/Statiq apps to confirm live status of chargers in smaller towns (Dhule, Guna, Rampur) before departure and again each morning — rural coverage is thinner than on the expressways.
- Carry a Type 2/CCS2 adapter and have both Tata Power and Statiq apps installed for redundancy.
- The final Haldwani → Bhowali → Kainchi Dham stretch is hill driving — expect higher consumption; charge to 90–100% at Haldwani before the climb.
