## 2. Gem-A — Foundation & Diploma (full detail, from official Gem-A specs)

### Gem-A Foundation in Gemmology

**Leads to:** Gem-A Level 4 Certificate in Gemmology (Ofqual 600/7103/5), UK
Regulated Qualifications Framework Level 4.
**Learning hours:** minimum 18 guided learning hours; total qualification
time up to 200 hours.
**Entry requirements:** No prior gemmology knowledge required. Students must
be 18+, and proficient (listening/speaking/writing) in the language of study
and assessment.

**Assessment structure:**

| Component | Mode | Duration | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assessed coursework | Coursework (4 blocks, online end-of-block assessments) | — | 10% |
| Practical endorsement | Coursework — instrument proficiency | — | Completion required |
| Foundation Paper 1 | Written exam — practical-based | 2 hours | 45% |
| Foundation Paper 2 | Written exam — theory | 2.5 hours | 45% |

**Grading:** A (80–100%) Pass · B (60–79%) Pass · C (40–59%) Re-sit final exam
· D (20–39%) Re-sit final exam · E (0–19%) Re-sit coursework and exam.

**Practical instruments covered:** crystal observation, 10× loupe,
refractometer, spectroscope, polariscope, dichroscope, Chelsea Colour Filter
(CCF), UV, conoscope, weight estimation, general observation/testing.

**Course content (4 blocks, 14 chapters):**
- **Block 1:** Gemmology and Gems · Observation and Magnification ·
  Materials and Structures · Durability
- **Block 2:** Weight, Density and Specific Gravity · Light and Optics ·
  Colour · Other Gem Properties
- **Block 3:** The Geology of Gems · The Gemstone Pipeline · Fashioning
  Gemstones · Gems and Jewellery
- **Block 4:** Gemstone Treatments · Artificial, Synthetic and Imitation Gem
  Materials · The Gemstones

**Gemstones on the Foundation syllabus:** Amber, Beryl, Chrysoberyl,
Corundum, Diamond, Feldspar, Fluorite, Garnet, Glass (artificial), Iolite,
Ivory, Jades (Pyroxene/Jadeite/Nephrite), Lapis Lazuli, Malachite, Opal,
Pearl, Peridot, Quartz, Spinel, Topaz, Tourmaline, Turquoise, Zircon,
Zoisite/Tanzanite.

**Progression:** on successful completion, students may continue to Gem-A's
Diploma in Gemmology.

### Gem-A Diploma in Gemmology

**Prerequisite:** Gem-A Foundation in Gemmology.
**Leads to:** Gem-A's "Diploma in Gemmology."

**Assessment structure** (theory and practical are separate components —
students must pass both):

| Component | Mode | Duration | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assessed coursework | Coursework (4 blocks) | — | 16% |
| Diploma Paper 1 | Written theory exam | 2h 30m | 42% |
| Diploma Paper 2 | Written theory exam | 2h 30m | 42% |
| Practical endorsement | Coursework — instrument proficiency | — | Completion required |
| Diploma Paper 3 | Written practical exam | 3h 15m | 100% of practical component |

**Grading:** A*/B* (80–100% / 60–79%) Pass · C/D (40–59% / 20–39%) Re-sit
final exam · E (0–19%) Re-sit coursework and exam. **Distinction** awarded
for A/A grades with zero stones wrong in the Paper 3 practical. **Merit**
awarded for an A grade plus an A or high-B (75%+) with one or fewer stones
wrong.

**Practical instruments/skills:** 10× lens and microscope, spectroscope,
dichroscope, CCF, polariscope, conoscope, refractometer, carat balance
(hydrostatic weighing), gauge, diamond probes/testers — across organic,
non-organic, ornamental, rough, fashioned, mounted, natural, artificial, and
treated stones.

**Course content (4 blocks, building on the Foundation syllabus):**
- **D1:** Structure and physical properties (Materials & Structure,
  Durability, Weight/Density/SG)
- **D2:** Magnification, light and colour
- **D3:** Treatments, synthetics and further testing techniques
- **D4:** The gemstone pipeline and the gemstones (geology, fashioning, and
  a much expanded gemstone list)

**Gemstones on the Diploma syllabus** (expanded from Foundation): Amber,
Andalusite, Apatite, Beryl, Calcite, Chrysoberyl, Coral, Corundum, Diamond,
Diopside, Feldspar, Fluorite, Garnet, Glass (natural), Gypsum, Hematite,
Horn, Iolite, Ivory, Jadeite, Jet, Kyanite, Lapis Lazuli, Nephrite,
Malachite, Opal, Peridot, Pearls, Prehnite, Pyrite, Rhodochrosite,
Rhodonite, Quartz, Scapolite, Serpentine, Shell, Sinhalite, Sodalite,
Sphene, Spinel, Spodumene, Steatite, Topaz, Tortoiseshell, Tourmaline,
Turquoise, Zircon, Zoisite (Tanzanite).

**Progression — the FGA pathway:** completing both Foundation and Diploma
qualifies a student to apply for **Fellowship of Gem-A (FGA)** — the
credential referenced across the GIVA/Reliance proposal work earlier. Beyond
that, Gem-A is a founding member of the Federation of European Education in
Gemmology (FEEG), which offers a further qualification to Diploma holders;
Birmingham City University's School of Jewellery also grants direct
third-year entry into its BSc (Hons) Gemmology and Jewellery to holders of
both the Gem-A Diploma and the Diploma in Gem Diamond.

## 3. Retail Training Programs — split into two tracks

Per your instruction: generic curriculum (not client-branded), Natural and
Lab-Grown kept as **separate tracks**, not one mixed program — this also
fixes the exact issue flagged in the very first review of the old manual.

### RTT — Natural Diamond Track
**Format:** 2-day program (Technical Foundation + Sales Application) for
sales associates and floor staff. A 4-day extended version is available,
adding a module on rarity, provenance, and the emotional value of natural
diamonds.

**Day 1 — Know Your Diamond (Technical Foundation):**
- Diamond Formation & Journey — Mine to Market
- The 4Cs — Cut: The Brilliance Driver
- The 4Cs — Color & Clarity
- The 4Cs — Carat Weight & Price Impact
- IGI Certification & Grading Report Literacy
- Diamond Treatments & Enhancements — What to Disclose
- Closing Quiz + Doubt Clearing

**Day 2 — Sell Your Diamond (Sales Application):**
- Customer Psychology — How Buyers Choose Diamonds
- The Language of Diamonds — Talking 4Cs on the Floor
- Objection Handling — Price, Online Comparison, Authenticity Concerns

**4-Day extension adds:** Rarity, Provenance & Emotional Value of Natural
Diamonds — a floor-ready module on what makes a natural stone's story part
of the sale.

### RTT — Lab-Grown Diamond Track
**Format:** 6-hour full-day program, 80% Technical Foundation + 20% Sales
Application. Max 25 per batch.

**7 Modules:**
1. **Introduction to Lab Grown Diamonds** — origin story, why LGDs matter,
   market context
2. **HPHT & CVD — The Science of Growth** — formation process, quality
   implications for retail selling
3. **4Cs for Lab Grown Diamonds** — Cut, Colour, Clarity, Carat, explained
   the way a customer needs to hear it
4. **Grading Reports & IGI Certification** — reading the cert, inscriptions,
   why it matters at point of sale
5. **LGD vs Natural vs Imitation** — key distinctions, positioned without
   undermining customer confidence
6. **Identification Basics & Disclosure** — handling detection questions,
   transparency standards
7. **Technical FAQs + Sales Application** — the 30 most common customer
   questions, practiced via role play
