Ages 12–16 · 5 days · 2.5 hrs/day

"The Gemstone Detective Files" — a real, miniaturized version of how gemology is actually taught, built around investigation rather than lectures. Real instruments, real gemstones, real IGI-credentialed instructors.

The 5 Days
  • Day 1 — What Makes a Gem a Gem? Sort real vs. fake stones, mineral vs. rock vs. organic gems, first time with a real 10× loupe.
  • Day 2 — The Diamond Code: Cracking the 4Cs. Flashlight-through-cut-stones for Cut, a color-matching game for Color, a loupe scavenger hunt for Clarity, guess-then-verify-on-a-scale for Carat.
  • Day 3 — Colored Stone Safari. Ruby, Emerald & Sapphire — why they're colored, a colored-stone ID challenge, a light touch on pearls.
  • Day 4 — Gem Forensics: Tools, Treatments & Fakes. Loupe, UV lamp, Chelsea Color Filter, natural vs. treated vs. synthetic vs. imitation.
  • Day 5 — The Gemstone Journey & Final Case File. A simplified mine-to-market story, then a solo mystery-stone investigation, presentations, and the certificate ceremony.
What they earn
Observer Badge 4Cs Codebreaker Badge Tool Master Badge

Every graduate receives the IGI Junior Gemologist Certificate, signed by their instructor and Head of Academics — a real, dual-signed keepsake, not a printout.
