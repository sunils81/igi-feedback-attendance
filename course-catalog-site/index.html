Gem-A · 01Foundation in Gemmology+
₹3,37,000 + GST
8 weeks · Mumbai campus only
Leads to

Gem-A Level 4 Certificate in Gemmology (Ofqual 600/7103/5) — UK Regulated Qualifications Framework, Level 4. 18+ guided learning hours, up to 200 hours total qualification time.

Course content — 4 blocks
  • Block 1 — Gemmology & Gems, Observation & Magnification, Materials & Structures, Durability
  • Block 2 — Weight/Density/SG, Light & Optics, Colour, Other Gem Properties
  • Block 3 — Geology of Gems, The Gemstone Pipeline, Fashioning, Gems & Jewellery
  • Block 4 — Treatments, Artificial/Synthetic/Imitation Materials, The Gemstones
Assessment
Assessed coursework10%
Practical endorsementCompletion required
Paper F1 — Practical exam2 hrs · 45%
Paper F2 — Theory exam2.5 hrs · 45%
Grading
A (80–100%) / B (60–79%)Pass
C (40–59%) / D (20–39%)Re-sit final exam
E (0–19%)Re-sit coursework + exam

No prior gemmology knowledge required. Minimum age 18. On completion, progress to the Gem-A Diploma.

Gem-A · 02Diploma in Gemmology+
₹4,99,000 + GST
12 weeks · Mumbai campus only · Requires Foundation
Course content — 4 blocks
  • D1 — Structure & physical properties (Materials, Durability, Weight/SG)
  • D2 — Magnification, light and colour
  • D3 — Treatments, synthetics, further testing techniques
  • D4 — The gemstone pipeline and the gemstones (expanded to 47 varieties)
Practical instruments

10× lens & microscope, spectroscope, dichroscope, CCF, polariscope, conoscope, refractometer, hydrostatic carat balance, gauge, diamond probes — across natural, artificial, and treated stones.

Assessment
Assessed coursework16%
Diploma Paper 1 — Theory2h 30m · 42%
Diploma Paper 2 — Theory2h 30m · 42%
Diploma Paper 3 — Practical3h 15m · 100% of practical
Grading
A*/B* (80–100% / 60–79%)Pass
C/D (40–59% / 20–39%)Re-sit final exam
Distinction / MeritAvailable — see below

Distinction: A/A grades, zero stones wrong in Paper 3. Merit: A + A/high-B (75%+), one or fewer stones wrong. Theory and practical are separate components — both must be passed.

The FGA pathway: completing both Foundation and Diploma qualifies you to apply for Fellowship of Gem-A (FGA). Gem-A is a founding member of the Federation of European Education in Gemmology (FEEG); Birmingham City University's School of Jewellery also grants direct third-year BSc entry to holders of both the Gem-A Diploma and Diploma in Gem Diamond.
