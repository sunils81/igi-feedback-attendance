Diopside, spodumene and andalusite

Diopside

RI 1.675–1.701, biref. 0.024–0.030, SG 3.29, H 5–6. Green from chromium; cat’s-eye and black star varieties.

Spodumene · lithium aluminium silicate

RI 1.660–1.676, SG 3.18, H 7. Kunzite (Mn, lilac pink; fades, strong orange LWUV glow) and hiddenite (Cr, green). Perfect cleavage, growth tubes.

Andalusite · orthorhombic

RI 1.634–1.644, biref. 0.010, SG 3.17, strongly pleochroic. Separate from tourmaline by its lower biref., higher SG and pleochroism. Chiastolite shows a dark cross.

What we learned

Feldspars

Moonstone (adularescence), labradorite (labradorescence), sunstone (aventurescence), amazonite.

Jade

Jadeite 1.66 / 3.33, granular. Nephrite 1.61 / 2.95, fibrous and tougher.

Collector gems

Kyanite two hardnesses; iolite strong pleochroism; kunzite fades; andalusite flashes red and green.
