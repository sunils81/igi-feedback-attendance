What is gemology?

From Latin gemma, a gem, and Greek logos, a talk: the science of gemstones.

It studies how gems form and where they occur, their properties, and how to identify synthetics and imitations.

Minerals

Ruby, sapphire, emerald, diamond and many more.

Organic matter

Minerals & crystals

A mineral is a homogeneous, naturally formed inorganic substance with a characteristic chemistry, a definite crystal structure, and fixed physical and optical properties.

Chemical composition · every mineral has a formula

C

Diamond: a single element, carbon.

How atoms arrange themselves

Amorphous

No orderly arrangement of atoms. Natural glass: obsidian, moldavite.

Crystalline

Orderly, three-dimensional, repeating arrangement: diamond, corundum, quartz. Atoms pack as closely as possible, in square (cubic) or hexagonal packing.

Crystallography · the science of crystals

Most gem rough is found as crystals or parts of crystals. Crystal form, cleavage and surface markings such as striations and etching all help identification.

Euhedral

Perfectly developed shape and faces.

Subhedral

Some faces better developed than others.

Anhedral

No properly developed faces, from growth or erosion.
