Luminescence = fluorescence + phosphorescence

Fluorescence

Visible light given off while a substance is excited by UV, X-rays, electrical discharge, heat or friction.

Phosphorescence

Glow that continues after the UV is switched off: the after-glow.

Terms

Fluorescence during excitation; phosphorescence after it; together, luminescence.

Lamps

LW 365 nm, SW 254 nm. Dark cabinet, eye protection.

Clues

Ruby red, diamond often blue, emerald weak, cobalt spinel red, zircon yellow in SW.
