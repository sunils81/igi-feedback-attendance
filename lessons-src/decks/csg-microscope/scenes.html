The Microscope & Inclusions

Inclusions are a gem’s biography: where it grew, how it grew, what it is, and whether nature or a laboratory made it.

The binocular gemmological microscope

Inclusions tell us

  • The growing environment
  • Sometimes the deposit
  • How the crystal grew
  • The mineral’s identity
  • Which individual stone it is
  • Natural, synthetic or treated
When did the inclusion form?
1

Protogenetic

Before the host. Minerals already present, trapped as the gem grew around them: crystals.

2

Syngenetic

At the same time as the host: growth tubes, negative crystals, fluid inclusions, colour zoning.

3

Epigenetic

After growth: healed fractures (fingerprints), fractures, tension haloes around expanding crystals.
