ADR

Anomalous double refraction: internal strain gives wriggling, snake-like bands. Synthetic spinel and glass.

What we learned

Filters

Aligned: light. Crossed: dark. Test with crossed filters and turn the stone.

Reactions

SR stays dark · DR blinks four times · aggregate stays light · ADR wriggles.

Figures

Uniaxial cross, quartz bull’s eye, biaxial “moustache”.
