Hardness · resistance to scratching · Mohs scale (relative, not even steps)
Toughness and stability

Toughness

Resistance to chipping and breaking. Emerald is hard but brittle: a carelessly used tool can break it.

Stability

Resistance to losing colour, dissolving in chemicals or other deterioration.

Hard is not the same as tough: jade (6–7) is far tougher than diamond (10).
Specific gravity = weight in air ÷ (weight in air − weight in water)

Weigh the stone in air, then suspended in water. The difference is the weight of water it pushes aside.

Specific gravity

Heavy liquids · drop a stone in each and watch

Diluted bromoform 2.65 (quartz floats in it as an indicator) · pure bromoform 2.88 · diluted methylene iodide 3.05 (tourmaline indicator) · pure methylene iodide 3.32. Lower SG floats, equal suspends, higher sinks; the faster it sinks, the bigger the difference. These liquids are toxic: handle only with lab safety.

Cleavage: breaks along definite directions, smooth surfaces · Fracture: breaks in any other direction

Heft

Weigh a loose stone in your hand: light, medium or heavy gives a rough idea of SG.

Colour starts with light

Two everyday sources

Daylight or fluorescent: white. Incandescent bulb: yellowish. A stone can look different under each.

What we see

Colour depends on the light source, how the gem absorbs and transmits it, and the human eye.

How colour is judged · hue, tone, saturation
Transparency: how much light passes through · Lustre: the quantity and quality of light reflected from the surface

Scale of transparency

Transparent · semi-transparent · translucent · semi-translucent · opaque
