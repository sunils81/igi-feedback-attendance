Spinel inclusions · and synthetic spinel

Synthetic spinel

Flame fusion: curved striations and gas bubbles. Synthetic blue spinel shows a chalky fluorescence under short-wave UV.

Synthetic spinel often shows anomalous double refraction (strain) in the polariscope; natural spinel is usually dark.
Lapis lazuli and fluorite

Lapis lazuli · a rock (aggregate)

RI about 1.50 (sometimes 1.67). Pyrite and calcite inclusions. Chalky white glow under UV from calcite. Often dyed or waxed. Best from Badakhshan, Afghanistan.

Fluorite · calcium fluoride

RI 1.434, SG 3.18, hardness only 4. Moderate to strong violet-blue fluorescence (the word “fluorescence” comes from fluorite).

Natural glass, man-made glass and plastic

Natural glass

RI about 1.49, SR, usually inert. Swirls and bubbles.

Man-made glass

RI 1.47–1.70 (or over the limit), SG 2.30–4.50. Gas bubbles, swirls, warm to touch.

Plastic

RI 1.46–1.70, SG only 1.05–1.55. Gas bubbles, flow lines; very light and warm.
