What heat does inside

Crystals melt and round off or change colour. They expand and crack the host, leaving disc-like tension haloes. Fingerprints stretch and alter. Rutile needles dissolve into dotted lines.

Glass-filled ruby · what to look for

Gas bubbles

Trapped in the glass, inside the fractures.

Flash effect

Bluish or orange flashes as you tilt the stone.

Lead glass is soft and can be damaged by jeweller’s heat, acids and even lemon juice. Always disclose it.
Heat can change colour completely

Amethyst → citrine

Most citrine on the market is heated amethyst.

Topaz

Heat removes brown; it is also used with irradiation to make blue topaz.

Tanzanite

Brownish zoisite heats to violet-blue. Almost all tanzanite is heated.

Porous and fragile gems · turquoise and opal

Turquoise

Waxing improves lustre, dyeing deepens colour, reconstruction bonds powder into a block.

Opal doublet and triplet

A thin opal slice on a dark backing (doublet), plus a clear quartz or plastic dome (triplet). Check the side.
