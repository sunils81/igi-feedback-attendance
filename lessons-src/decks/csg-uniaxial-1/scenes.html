Reminder · on the refractometer

Two shadow edges, one never moves

1

Two readings

Light splits into an ordinary ray (ω) with a constant RI and an extraordinary ray (ε) that varies as you rotate.

2

Birefringence

Highest minus lowest reading. Beryl about 0.006, tourmaline about 0.020, zircon about 0.059.

3

Optic sign

If ε is higher than ω the stone is positive (+), e.g. quartz, zircon. If lower, negative (−), e.g. beryl, tourmaline, corundum.
