Crystalline

One crystal: DR. Under the polariscope with a conoscope it shows a bull’s-eye interference figure.

Polycrystalline

Countless tiny crystals: an aggregate (AGG). Stays light in every position. RI about 1.53–1.54, SG about 2.60.

Key separation: RI, SG and, for crystalline quartz, the bull’s-eye figure.

Crystalline quartz

Inclusions

Zebra stripes and colour zoning in amethyst; rutile and tourmaline needles.

Polycrystalline

Chalcedony even, agate banded, jasper opaque. Often dyed.
