Sorting for size

Section 02

Clarity sorting

Small stones get split grades, judged on the face-up appearance. The same five parameters still apply: nature, size, number, position, colour.

Clarity split grades · always given face-up
VVSVery very small inclusions · minute
VSVery small inclusions · minor
SISmall inclusions · noticeable
I1Large and/or numerous · obviousblocks up to 15%
I2Large and/or numerousblocks > 15%
I3Heavily includedblocks > 50%
RRejection: chipped, broken, or not a diamondblocks > 75–80%
For I grades, don’t look for naked-eye visibility in tiny stones: judge light blockage.
A chipped culet in a star or melee falls under VS.

Section 03

Colour sorting

Judge colour with the naked eye, looking through the pavilion. Brown-tinged stones use the same grades as yellow.

Colour split grades
DescriptionTrade termGrade
ColourlessWhiteD E F
Near colourlessOff whiteG H
Slightly tintedTTLC / TTLBI J
Very light yellow / brownTLC / TLBK L M
Light yellow / brownLC / LBN – Z
Dark yellow / brown & fancyDC / DBFancy

TTLC = top top light cape; TLC = top light cape; LC = light cape; DC = dark cape (B = brown). Know them, but record the letter grades on your sheet.
