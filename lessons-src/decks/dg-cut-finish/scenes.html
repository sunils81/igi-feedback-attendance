Finish = polish + symmetry. Cut = proportions + finish.

Section 02

Symmetry

Symmetry is about balance: the shape and size of facets, the position of table and culet, the outline.

Types of symmetry
One line

Pear, heart, triangle, tapered baguette.

Two lines

Marquise, oval, cushion, princess, emerald, radiant, Asscher, baguette.

Four lines

Rounds. Visually divide the stone into eight sections and compare them.

Judge the overall effect face-up: one tiny open facet is not the same as a table that is visibly off centre.

Section 03

Proportion grade

Grade every parameter on the chart. The lowest grade is your final grade.

IGI cut grade chart for round brilliants

Parameters

Total depth, crown angle, table, crown height, girdle, pavilion depth, pavilion angle, polish and symmetry.

Ideal column

Everything in the centre column, with polish and symmetry at least Very Good: Excellent, comment “Ideal Cut Round Brilliant”.

Watch for

A fish-eye caps the grade at Fair. A girdle range may not span more than one grade.

What is hearts and arrows?

A test of optical symmetry

Seen through a hearts-and-arrows viewer, a round with excellent symmetry and ideal angles shows 8 hearts from the pavilion and 8 arrows from the crown.

What we learned

Polish

Polish lines, burns, scratches, abrasions, nicks. Abraded or chipped culet = Good.

Symmetry

1, 2 or 4 lines. T o/c, C o/c, ALN, IRR, open and short facets, Ptg, OR, N, EF; UW, US, UL, UC, SP for fancies.

Proportion grade

Every parameter on the chart; the lowest wins. Fish-eye caps at Fair. All ideal = Excellent, Ideal Cut.

Hearts and arrows

8 hearts from the pavilion, 8 arrows from the crown.
