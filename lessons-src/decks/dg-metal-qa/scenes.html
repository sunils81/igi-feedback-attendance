What the inspector checks

Metal & finish

  • Even prongs
  • Porosity
  • Assembly / locking system
  • Broken links
  • Even polish
  • Scratches / tool marks
  • Post bend
  • Prong over-polish
  • Shank thickness
  • Size
  • Weight

Stones & setting

  • Smaller diamond used
  • Bigger diamond used
  • Chipped diamond
  • Tilted stone
  • Improper setting
  • Improper cut of shape
  • Miracle plate shape off
  • Missing stone
  • Loose stone
  • Colour-stone mismatch
