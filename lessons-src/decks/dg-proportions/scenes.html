Section 01

Crown angle

The angle between the girdle plane and the bezel facets. It controls dispersion: the fire.

Crown-angle visualiser · profile, reflected light, 10x
Crown angle
34.5°

Profile method · take all eight bezels
1

Read each bezel

Turn the stone to bring each of the 8 bezel facets into profile.

2

Average them

Sum of readings ÷ number of readings.

3

Check the spread

Max minus min of 3° or more: write Crn Ang Var in the symmetry section.

Face-up method · darkfield, 10x

Watch the pavilion mains enter the bezels

Find the 8 pavilion mains (follow them out from the culet). Where each enters a bezel facet, its reflection changes width.

Shallower crown → smoother line

Steeper crown → wider reflection

Pros

All 8 readings at once, without turning the stone.

Cons

Only for stones without heavy inclusions or symmetry problems. Need at least 4 readable bezels.

Section 03

Crown height %

The distance from the table to the top of the girdle, as a percentage. Tolkowsky: 16.2%. We work it out from the crown angle and the table.

Crown height from crown angle and table

CH% = tan(crown angle) × (100 − table%) ÷ 2

No calculator in the lab? The table on page 63 of your notes gives the same answers.

Section 04

Pavilion depth %

From the bottom of the girdle to the culet. It controls total internal reflection. Tolkowsky: 43.1% (pavilion angle 40.75°). Market: 42–45%.

What the pavilion does to light
Too shallow

Fish eye

Light leaks out of the pavilion. Grey, watery look with a ring of girdle reflection.

Normal

Total internal reflection

Light bounces off both pavilion halves and comes back up through the crown.

Too deep

Nail head

Light escapes through the far side. A dark centre.

See it face-up
Fish eye · 39–41%Normal · 42–45%Nail head · 46–49%
Pavilion-depth estimator · face-up through the table, darkfield, 10x · culet centred
Pavilion depth

Alternative method · work it out

PD% = TD% − (CH% + girdle%)

Girdle% = (min% + max%) ÷ 2

Example

TD 61.0%, CH 14.0%, girdle 3.5% → PD = 61.0 − 17.5 = 43.5%

Crown angle · fire

Shallow ≤31°, normal 32–36°, steep ≥37°. 8 readings; 3° spread = Crn Ang Var.

Table · brilliance

Quick flash, bowing (straight edge 61–62%), ratio (1:1 = 54%, 1:2 = 72%).

tan(CA) × (100 − T) ÷ 2.

Pavilion depth · return of light
