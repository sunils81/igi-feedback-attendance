Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Hardness

Station 1

Mohs order

  1. Ten unlabelled rough minerals.
  2. Arrange them 1 to 10 using what you know.
  3. Check against the key.
Station 2

Scratch test (rough only)

  1. Mentor-supervised: on rough minerals only.
  2. Glass plate vs quartz vs fluorite.
  3. Record what scratches what.
Station 3

Wear on cut stones

  1. Five worn stones from old jewellery.
  2. Facet edges: sharp or rounded?
  3. Rank by likely hardness.
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Toughness, fracture, cleavage

Station 4

Fracture types

  1. Obsidian, quartz, nephrite, an uneven fragment.
  2. Name each fracture.
  3. Sketch one.
Station 5

Cleavage

  1. Fluorite octahedron, topaz, calcite.
  2. Count cleavage directions.
  3. Find an incipient cleavage in a cut stone.
Station 6

Ring or not?

  1. Topaz, opal, sapphire, nephrite, zircon, emerald.
  2. Rate hardness and toughness.
  3. Which in a daily ring?
