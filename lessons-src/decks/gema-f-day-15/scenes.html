Answering well

Most lost marks are misread questions, not missing knowledge.

Online questions

Six habits

  • Read the whole question and every option before choosing.
  • Underline the key word: not, most, best, always.
  • Eliminate options you know are wrong.
  • Look for the precise term: gemstone vs gem material, toughness vs hardness.
  • Don't spend too long on one question: mark it and return.
  • Use any time left to check, not to change answers at random.
Your turn · spot the trap, then click

Classic traps

"A synthetic gemstone"
Trap

Contradiction

"Gemstone" is natural only: say "synthetic ruby" or "synthetic gem material".

"Diamond is the toughest gem"
Trap

Hard ≠ tough

Diamond is the hardest; nephrite is the toughest.

"Quartz is hexagonal"
Trap

Trigonal

Six-sided prism, but trigonal symmetry.

"Opal is a crystal"
Trap

Amorphous

Ordered spheres, but no crystal lattice.

"Lapis lazuli is a mineral"
Trap

A rock

An aggregate of several minerals.

"Cubic zirconia is zircon"
Trap

Different material

CZ is synthetic; zircon is a natural silicate.

Gem of the Day · 15 of 60

Jadeite and nephrite

JadeitePyroxene (sodium aluminium silicate); H 7; SG about 3.33; RI about 1.66NephriteAmphibole (tremolite–actinolite); H 6–6½; SG about 2.95; RI about 1.61StructureBoth polycrystalline aggregates: AGG in the polariscopeToughnessExceptional: nephrite the toughest of all gemsSourcesJadeite: Myanmar. Nephrite: China, Canada, New Zealand, RussiaTreatment"B jade": bleached and polymer-impregnated; "C jade": dyed
"Jade" = two materialsImperial green (jadeite)Pounamu (NZ nephrite)

Only in 1863 were "jade" objects shown to be two different minerals, jadeite and nephrite. Chinese carvers worked nephrite for thousands of years before jadeite arrived from Burma.

Theory · 4 marks

Explain why both jadeite and nephrite are exceptionally tough, and how you could separate them without damage.

Two marks for toughness, two for separation.

1Both are polycrystalline aggregates of interlocking crystals.
1Jadeite: granular crystals; nephrite: felted fibres; cracks cannot run straight through.
1SG: jadeite about 3.33 vs nephrite about 2.95.
1RI (spot method): jadeite about 1.66 vs nephrite about 1.61.
