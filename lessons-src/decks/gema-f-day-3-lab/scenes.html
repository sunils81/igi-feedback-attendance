Bench rules: clean every stone before you look; tweezers by the girdle; one stone out of its paper at a time; work low over the tray.
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Colour, lustre, transparency

Station 1 · colour

Describe five stones

  1. On white, in white light.
  2. Hue, tone and saturation for each.
  3. Find one with colour zoning on the flat light.
Station 2 · lustre

Rank them

  1. Reflect light off the table facet of each stone.
  2. Rank from highest to lowest lustre.
  3. Name each lustre type.
Station 3 · transparency

Through the light

  1. Hold each stone to the light.
  2. Transparent, translucent, semi-translucent or opaque?
  3. Note one stone where it was hard to decide.
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Cut, size, effects

Station 4 · cut

Shape and style

  1. Name shape and style of cut for six stones.
  2. Count the crown facets on a round brilliant.
  3. Find a mixed cut: what is on the crown, what on the pavilion?
Station 5 · size

Measure

  1. Use the mm ruler.
  2. Record L × W × D; for rounds, two diameters and depth.
  3. Compare with your partner: how close are you?
Station 6 · effects

Find the effect

  1. Black background, pinpoint light.
  2. Find star, cat's eye, play of colour, adularescence.
  3. Rotate the stone: does the effect move?
