Bench rules: clean every stone before you look; tweezers by the girdle; one stone out of its paper at a time; work low over the tray.
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

With the loupe

Station 1 · surface

Read the surface

  1. Reflected light.
  2. Facet edges: sharp, rounded or abraded?
  3. Find polishing lines, scratches, chips.
Station 2 · inside

Find an inclusion

  1. Stone at the edge of the light.
  2. Describe one inclusion: shape, number, size, colour.
  3. Is it one, two or three phases?
Station 3 · doubling

Hunt the doubling

  1. Look through the table of a peridot or zircon at the back facet edges.
  2. Compare with a garnet.
  3. Which shows doubled edges?
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

With the microscope

Station 4 · set-up

Three lightings

  1. Darkfield: find an inclusion.
  2. Switch to brightfield: what changes?
  3. Top light: examine the surface.
Station 5 · sketch

Draw two inclusions

  1. Crown-view outline with facets.
  2. Place each inclusion where it is.
  3. Label using the conventions.
Station 6 · composites

Spot the join

  1. Examine a doublet from the side.
  2. Find the junction line and the change of lustre.
  3. Look for flattened bubbles in the join.
