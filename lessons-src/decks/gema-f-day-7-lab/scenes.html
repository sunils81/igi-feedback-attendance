Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Models

Station 1

Find the axes

  1. Take the cube, tetragonal and hexagonal models.
  2. Push skewers (or point) along the reference axes.
  3. Equal or unequal? At 90° or 120°?
Station 2

Count the symmetry

  1. Rotate each model about its c-axis.
  2. How many times does it repeat in one turn?
  3. Record 2-, 3-, 4- or 6-fold.
Station 3

Odd ones out

  1. Orthorhombic, monoclinic and triclinic models.
  2. Which have inclined axes?
  3. Which has no symmetry axis?
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Crystals

Station 4

Name the system

  1. Eight numbered crystals.
  2. Use the cross-section and shape.
  3. Write system and one reason.
Station 5

Cross-sections

  1. Look down the length of each prism.
  2. Square, triangle, hexagon or rhomb?
  3. Sketch each section.
Station 6

Gem examples

  1. Match ten gem cards to the seven systems.
  2. Check with the table.
  3. Which system has the most gems?
