Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Describe

Station 1

Five steps, crystal A

  1. Habit, cross-section, terminations, surface, twins and colour.
  2. Write each step.
  3. Conclude: "consistent with…".
Station 2

Five steps, crystal B

  1. Same method.
  2. Compare with your partner.
  3. Agree on the system.
Station 3

Rough or pebble?

  1. Water-worn sapphire, spinel, garnet pebbles.
  2. What survives? What is lost?
  3. What else would you need to test?
Bench stations · rotate on your mentor's signal

Sketch and identify

Station 4

Sketch one crystal

  1. Large, c-axis first, ruler.
  2. Striations in their true direction.
  3. Label everything.
Station 5

Identify six

  1. Six numbered crystals.
  2. Name gem and system.
  3. One key clue each.
Station 6

Natural or synthetic?

  1. A synthetic boule, a natural crystal.
  2. What differs in shape and surface?
  3. Why is shape alone not proof?
