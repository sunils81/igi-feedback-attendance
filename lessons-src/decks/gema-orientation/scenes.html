Why Gem-A?

Because for more than a century, FGA has been the letters the gem trade trusts.

A legacy since 1908

The world's longest-established gem education provider

1908The beginning

The UK's National Association of Goldsmiths sets up a committee to create gemmology qualifications.

1913First diploma

The first gemmology diploma ever awarded. FGA traces its line to it.

1931Gem-A formed

The Gemmological Association is established in its own right, for education and research.

TodayWorldwide

Students and Fellows across the world; accredited teaching centres on several continents, including IGI Mumbai.

What that means for you: you are not buying a course certificate. You are sitting the same independent examination that has defined the profession for over a century.

Gem-A, "Gem-A's History", gem-a.com

What it takes · eight habits of students who pass
0

teaching days, 26 Oct – 24 Dec

0

hours of class and lab, before revision

0

chapters, 4 blocks, 20+ gem families

  • Treat it as a full-time commitment. Plan other work and travel around the class calendar.
  • Study every evening. We recommend 1½ – 2 hours of self-study a day on top of class.
  • Read the chapter before the class, then again after it.
  • Practise until it is muscle memory. Refractometer, polariscope, spectroscope: speed and accuracy come only from repetition.
  • Keep a lab notebook. Write down every stone you test and every reading you take.
  • Revise every week, not just before the exam. Each block builds on the last.
  • Never miss a lab. Practical skills cannot be caught up from notes.
  • Ask. If something is unclear today, it will be harder next week.

Hours: 39 days × 4½ teaching hours (10 am – 4 pm less lunch and two breaks). Self-study time is IGI's recommendation.

Exam day · 19 or 20 January 2027 · IGI Mumbai

Two papers. One day. An external invigilator.

  • Arrive early. Late arrivals may not be allowed in.
  • Bring photo ID and the exam details Gem-A sends you.
  • Phones and smart watches switched off and handed in.
  • Only the instruments and materials you are told you may use.
  • The invigilator's instructions are final. No talking once the paper starts.
  • Eat and rest between the papers: it is a long day.

IGI guidance. Gem-A's examination instructions take precedence.
