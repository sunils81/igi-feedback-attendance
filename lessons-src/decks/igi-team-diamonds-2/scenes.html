Why does the scale start at D, not A?

A fresh start

Older systems used A, B, C, AAA and more, inconsistently. The modern scale deliberately began at D so it would not be confused with them.

🧪
What makes a diamond yellowish?

Nitrogen

A few nitrogen atoms among millions of carbon atoms absorb blue light, so the stone looks yellow. Boron makes diamonds blue, like the Hope.

🌈
Do coloured diamonds follow D–Z?

No: "fancy colour"

Pink, blue, green or intense yellow diamonds are graded on a separate fancy-colour scale, where more colour usually means more value.

Fluorescence · it is on every IGI report

Some diamonds glow under ultraviolet light

Fluorescence is light a diamond gives off under ultraviolet. Many natural diamonds fluoresce, usually blue. It is a characteristic, not a defect, and in daylight you normally cannot see it.

On the report

IGI reports fluorescence as NONE, VERY SLIGHT or SLIGHT, through MEDIUM, STRONG and VERY STRONG.

Strong blue fluorescence can make a slightly yellowish stone look whiter, and occasionally makes a very white stone look a little hazy. That's why it's recorded.

Find the four inclusions

  • Crystal · a tiny mineral trapped inside
  • Feather · a small internal crack
  • Cloud · a group of tiny pinpoints
  • Needle · a long thin crystal

Graders scan the whole stone in a pattern, at 10×, so nothing is missed.

Now the twist: these features are why the IGI report includes a clarity plot. Like a fingerprint, they help identify that exact stone.

Where does the word "carat" come from?

Carob seeds

Traders once balanced gems against the seeds of the carob tree. Today one metric carat is exactly 0.2 grams (200 milligrams).

💯
What is a "pointer"?

1 carat = 100 points

A 0.25 ct diamond is a "25-pointer". The trade often talks in points for small stones.

⚖️
Carat or karat?

Different things!

Carat (ct) is the weight of a gem. Karat (K, as in 22K or 18K) is the purity of gold. Mixing them up in a post is a classic mistake.

Chapter 04

Cut: the C made by people

Nature decides colour, clarity and size. Only cut is in the hands of the cutter, and it decides how much the diamond sparkles.

How IGI grades cut

Proportions, polish and symmetry, rolled into one grade

IDEALThe top grade IGI gives to the best-proportioned round brilliants on laboratory-grown diamond reports.
EXCELLENTThe top grade on natural diamond reports; well-made rounds often carry the comment "IDEAL CUT ROUND BRILLIANT".
VERY GOODBright, with slightly less ideal proportions.
GOODSome light is lost; often cut to save weight.
FAIR · POORNoticeably dull or dark areas.

Polish & symmetry

Two separate grades, Excellent to Poor. Polish: how smooth the facets are. Symmetry: how precisely they line up.

Fancy shapes too

Most labs give a cut grade to rounds only. In 2022 IGI became the first lab to grade the cut of fancy shapes like ovals and pears.

Chapter 06

Say it right

Accurate words protect the customer, the brand and IGI. A few simple rules for every post, brochure, email and phone call.

Six rules for anything a customer will read

Write it like a grader would

✓ Quote grades exactly as the report shows them

"G colour, VS1 clarity" — never round up to "near flawless" or "D-quality look".

✓ Always disclose laboratory-grown, in full

"Laboratory-grown diamond" or "laboratory-created diamond", every time it is named.

✓ Use "flawless" only for FL

IF is "internally flawless". Nothing else is flawless.

✕ No green or "nature" claims for laboratory-grown

Avoid "earth-friendly", "eco", "pure", "nature’s", "cultured".

✕ Don’t mix carat and karat

Carat (ct) = gem weight. Karat (K) = gold purity.

✕ Don’t promise value

No "investment", "guaranteed to appreciate" or resale claims. We grade; we don’t price.
