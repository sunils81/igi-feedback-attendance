Your Next Step

Beyond Graduate Gemologist

Finishing your Diamond Graduate / Colored Stone Graduate training is a real milestone — here's a path worth knowing about for where it can take you next.

Why the FGA (Gem-A Gemmology Diploma)?

Gem-A was established in 1908, making the FGA one of the oldest and most respected professional gemmology qualifications in the world. It isn't just another course — graduates earn the internationally recognised post-nominal designation FGA, a credential that becomes part of your professional identity, recognised by laboratories, jewellery manufacturers, retailers, auction houses and educators across Europe, the Middle East, Asia and beyond.

🌍 The Gateway to Europe

The FGA is the approved pathway to the European Gemmologist (FEEG) qualification — a credential with strong recognition specifically across Europe. For a gemologist thinking about an international career, this is the concrete, documented route there, not just a general claim of "global recognition."

Questions students usually ask
Why not a shorter course?
A shorter course teaches the basics. The FGA develops internationally recognised professional competence — a real credential, not just a certificate of attendance.
Is it recognised outside India?
Yes — the FGA is recognised by employers and institutions across many countries, and is the documented pathway to FEEG in Europe specifically.
Will it actually help my career?
Yes — it opens opportunities across laboratories, retail, manufacturing, trading, valuation, education and running your own business, backed by a credential employers already recognise.
